This wiki covers using and configuring UAMSWP Block Remover: removing blocks from the editor, hiding embed providers, running it on a multisite network, and what to do when something does not behave as expected. The [README](https://github.com/UAMS-Web/uamswp-block-remover/blob/main/README.md) keeps the overview, requirements, installation and development notes.

- [Removing your first block](Removing-your-first-block.md): open the settings page, remove a block, and see it gone from the editor.

**Guides**

- [Hiding embed providers](Hiding-embed-providers.md): keep the Embed block but hide providers such as TikTok or Spotify.
